First, neuron variables are initialized (x, r, nspikes). x is the neuron internal state of shape (N,), r is the neuron output of shape (N,), nspikes is the matrix of whether spikes occurred or not of shape (N, T). Then, Iext of shape (N, T) is initialized with 200 ms of external injected current in [-A, A]. Connectivity matrix is initialized with probability of an entry being nonzero=0.3, no autapses, the mean of each row is zero, overall variance is set by gsyn and connection probability p.

The whole experiment is across 16 loop iterations.
One iteration have an inner loop of itmax = 1000 (ms) iterations. Each iteration of this loop is it = 1 ms. T now represents the 1000 ms. Each iteration uses Iext that stores external current of shape (N, T), where the first 200 columns (which then corresponds to 200ms) is the injected external current in [-A, A], and the remaining columns are containers for synaptic drive (the recurrent input W*r).
